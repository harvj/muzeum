# v0.5.0 — Canonical Release Context

A verified recording can appear on multiple canonical releases. For a recording with eight observations on both a single and an album, the recording, single, album and combined history each count eight observations. Combined histories query the union of member recordings and count each effective observation once. The original scrobbles and individual overrides are preserved.

Recording confirmation automatically queues verification of supported release memberships. A verified MusicBrainz tracklist establishes catalog membership. The played edition remains unknown. Incomplete listings and conflicting original track sequences remain pending. Where original editions share the same complete opening sequence and differ only by extra final tracks, use the shortest verified tracklist for the base collection and retain the longer edition as evidence. Remove the separate edition confirmation screen.

The Artist page presents estimated listening time, range-filtered history, recordings ranked by play count, and releases ranked by estimated time. Verified featured credits participate in artist history. All contexts derive their metrics from distinct effective observations and current canonical recording durations. Plays without a known positive duration still count, with duration coverage shown alongside estimates.
