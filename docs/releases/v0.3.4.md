# v0.3.4 — Resolution Evidence and Artist Enrichment

Muzeum’s Resolution Review makes it easier to compare recordings and see what each decision will affect. The original Last.fm track, artist and album claims are labeled separately from catalog evidence. Expanded candidates show retained disc-aware tracklists, printed track numbers, durations and the matching recording. Checked catalog appearances contribute equally to new scores regardless of the album caption. Historical scores remain available with their original factors explained.

The review distinguishes supporting evidence, conflicts and uncertainty. Score details open inline and each confirmation states whether it will affect one observation or the matching group. Reject and defer remain separate actions. Catalog appearances establish where recordings are listed. The edition played remains a separate decision.

Artist refreshes can now retain verified Wikidata geographic evidence alongside MusicBrainz. Origins include Scotland for Boards of Canada and Chicago for Wilco, with their own Wikimedia Commons flags and the relevant country hierarchy. Where a flag or a specific origin cannot be verified, the name or country fallback remains readable.

The global wordmark and footer credit link to Muzeum and harvj.com. The signed-out **Log In** link opens Muzeum’s canonical `/login`. An existing valid session opens the archive. New sign-ins go directly through Auth and return to their validated protected destination when applicable. The old intermediate sign-in page is retired.

This candidate includes the Ruby 3.4.13 security patch and Brakeman 8.1.1. It requires a separately reviewed Muzeum production runtime handoff. There are no application schema migrations. [Ruby security advisory](https://www.ruby-lang.org/en/news/2026/11/18/multiple-vulnerabilities-in-regexp-and-string/)
