## Songs: Ohia · Greenbrier

- [Recording 37a4c6c0-a300-44d5-88fb-0dbc40c790f5](https://musicbrainz.org/recording/37a4c6c0-a300-44d5-88fb-0dbc40c790f5) returned one exact release match. The [2004 US vinyl](https://musicbrainz.org/release/37969e2b-b9bf-4fda-bf8b-5c58000ffdbb) contains it on medium 1, position 11, printed B4, track MBID 7ed2d01d-6f27-4469-9b25-7b351491b437. The release group is a6f46600-0abd-3d52-85c8-6040d008e7a2. MusicBrainz credits Songs: Ohia and supplies no ISRC, duration, recording date or venue. The [work](https://musicbrainz.org/work/f0741e32-26b1-4761-aa67-37b9623e0281) currently links only this recording.
- The [1997 CD](https://musicbrainz.org/release/78569136-057d-48b6-83a6-96a7eea54977) has 13 tracks and omits Greenbrier. Preserved reissue copy says the bonus song was on earlier vinyl pressings and that the record was out of print on vinyl by mid-1998. A separate record-shop listing describes a March 1997 US SC4 LP with Greenbrier at B4. These support a pre-2004 vinyl appearance for the song. The precise publication date and the identity of the audio on that earlier pressing remain unverified. [Reissue copy and track list](https://lunchboxrecords.com/collections/music/products/656605000411) · [Earlier pressing listing](https://www.potenterecords.com/en/products/songs-ohia-songs-ohia)
- **Interpretation:** 2004 is the earliest checked appearance of this exact MBID. Evidence supports an earlier appearance of the song and leaves exact recording identity and original publication open.

The [2007 US CD + CD-R edition](https://musicbrainz.org/release/90a206e9-968b-426b-ada8-e4ef190e4eac) is dated only to the year. The [11-track July 9 CD](https://musicbrainz.org/release/18484ef2-8dc4-30b1-8618-85950a374b1d) supplies the base comparison. MusicBrainz credits Rich Costey and Interpol as producers of the album recording “Pace Is the Trick,” with Rich Costey as mixer and Electric Lady and The Magic Shop as engineering locations. The instrumental has no corresponding studio, producer or session credits in the retrieved evidence. [Album recording](https://musicbrainz.org/recording/17241ce7-07c6-4de9-a5af-822cf2b61000) · [Artist release](https://interpol.bandcamp.com/album/our-love-to-admire)

A shared work and the instrumental relationship attribute support an alternate version of the composition. They leave open whether the source was the original multitrack, a different mix, or a separate performance. The orchestral-with-vocals caption and shared work support an orchestral version as an interpretation. Its mix and performance relationship are unclassified.
